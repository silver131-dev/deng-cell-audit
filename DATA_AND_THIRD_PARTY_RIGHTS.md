## Provider data and real NTC statistics

Official datasets remain subject to their provider's terms even after they have been downloaded or a challenge has ended. This repository grants no permission to obtain restricted data, share accounts or download links, bypass access controls, or redistribute a provider's data. It includes no data-download or authentication client.

A future release that adds any real NTC fingerprint or panel-context table must complete a **separate data review before inclusion**. Record:

1. The exact proposed files, hashes, source dataset/version, units, and aggregation/transformation steps.
2. The applicable source terms and their version/date, rights holder, and the specific basis permitting the proposed distribution. Check derived/aggregate outputs explicitly; permission to access raw data does not itself establish redistribution permission.
3. Any third-party attribution, notices, privacy, contractual, or institutional requirements relevant to those files.
4. The reviewer, review date, concrete allowed scope, and any remaining restrictions. Unresolved permission means the data remain excluded.
5. A separate data-license/rights statement and an updated reviewed file list and manifest. Do not silently extend the software's MIT statement to the data.

This is a distribution review, separate from numerical correctness, biological validity, source authentication, or a `MODEL_USE_HOLD` decision. Passing automated tests does not grant data rights or certify compliance with a competition's submission rules.
